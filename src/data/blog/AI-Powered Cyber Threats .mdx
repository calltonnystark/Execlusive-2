# AI-Powered Cyber Threats

## Overview

AI-powered chatbots aur virtual assistants ab hospitals, help desks aur
critical digital systems mein extensively use ho rahe hain.

In systems ke barhne ke sath unke cybersecurity risks bhi increase ho
rahe hain.

## Key Threats

- Data leakage
- AI-assisted phishing
- Social engineering
- Manipulation of AI/ML systems
- Sensitive information exposure
- Model-level attacks

## Why Traditional Security Is Not Enough

Traditional controls, jaise:

- Firewalls
- Passwords
- Antivirus

modern AI systems ke tamam attack surfaces ko adequately address nahi
karte.

AI systems complex data process karte hain aur attackers AI-generated
content aur model manipulation jaisi techniques use kar sakte hain.

## Security Strategy

### 1. Strong Encryption

Sensitive information ko encryption ke through protect karna chahiye.

### 2. Access Control

AI systems aur sensitive data ke liye strict authentication aur
authorization implement karni chahiye.

### 3. Adversarial Training

Models ko adversarial inputs ke against evaluate aur train karna chahiye.

### 4. Anomaly Detection

Unusual behavior aur suspicious activity ko identify karne ke liye
intelligent monitoring use karni chahiye.

### 5. Governance

AI deployment ke liye:

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Privacy
- Regulatory compliance
- Ethical controls

ko security architecture ka part banana chahiye.

## Conclusion

AI-powered systems ko secure karna future concern nahi balki current
cybersecurity requirement hai.
